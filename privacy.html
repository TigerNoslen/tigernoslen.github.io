Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Tiger Nation HQ is operated by Nelson Pinheiro. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website or sign in with Google.

Google sign-in

When you sign in with Google, Google provides basic account information, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier. We use this information to create and maintain your website account and display your signed-in identity.

We do not receive your Google password. Our sign-in feature does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or contacts.

Account storage

We use Supabase to handle authentication and store account information. Sign-in session information is stored in your browser so you can remain signed in when you refresh or return to the website.

Signing out ends your website session; it does not delete your account.

Website services and external links

The website uses GitHub Pages for hosting and Cloudflare for website services. Your browser also requests resources from external providers. These providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, when handling requests.

Links to services such as YouTube, Twitch, and Discord lead to websites governed by their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Account information is retained while your website account remains active. To request account deletion, email tiger.noslen@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.

Deletion may not immediately remove information retained in service-provider backups or records required for security or legal purposes.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, contact tiger.noslen@gmail.com.

We may update this policy as the website changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
